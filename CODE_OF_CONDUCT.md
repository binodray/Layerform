# Community code of conduct

Layer Form should be a useful, welcoming project for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific and constructive.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent while still asking for evidence and reproducible details.
- Respect privacy and do not post personal information, credentials or private project files.
- Give credit for upstream work, research, design and contributions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, deliberate disruption, impersonation and publishing another person's private information are not accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its community. Serious concerns can be reported privately through the repository's security contact. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential where practical.
